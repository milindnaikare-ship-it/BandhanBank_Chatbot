User Journeys & Use Cases

How real customers move through the assistant — end to end.

A walkthrough for client conversations: who the users are, the journeys they complete, and the full catalogue of use cases the assistant delivers today.

Persona A

Prospective Visitor

Not yet a customer. Exploring products, rates and eligibility — often on mobile, often in a regional language. Wants quick answers and an easy way to express interest.

Persona B

Existing Customer

Has an account, registered mobile and debit card. Wants to complete a service task fast — without a branch visit, a call, or the full net-banking portal.

The Experience

Seven signature journeys

Each is a live, demonstrable flow. Numbers show the customer's steps in order.

1

Discover a product & express interest

Prospective Visitor · acquisition
  • Lands on the bank site and opens the assistant from the floating button.
  • Asks "What savings accounts do you offer?" — gets a clear, table-formatted comparison drawn from the bank's own content.
  • Follows up on interest rates and eligibility; answers stay accurate and on-brand.
  • Says "I want to open an account" — the assistant captures name, mobile & interest as a lead.
Outcome: a qualified lead captured 24×7, with the customer's questions already answered.
2

Get help in my own language — by voice

Either persona · accessibility & inclusion
  • Selects Hindi, Bengali, Marathi or Hinglish before starting.
  • Taps the mic and speaks the question instead of typing.
  • The assistant replies in the same language, and reads the answer aloud.
  • Continues the conversation naturally, switching between voice and text.
Outcome: reaches customers underserved by English-only, text-only channels.
3

Securely identify myself

Existing Customer · authentication
  • Chooses "Existing customer".
  • Enters registered mobile number.
  • Receives and enters an OTP to verify.
  • Enters the authenticated experience, greeted by name.
Outcome: a trusted, gated session before any sensitive action.
4

Reset my debit-card PIN

Existing Customer · card servicing
  • Opens Self-service Banking and picks the debit card (blocked/expired cards are declined).
  • Confirms identity with card expiry and an OTP.
  • Enters a new PIN twice on a secure keypad — never shown in the chat.
  • Receives success with a service reference number.
Outcome: an ATM/branch trip avoided; a common call-centre driver deflected.
5

Add a payee & send money

Existing Customer · payments
  • Adds a beneficiary: name, account (entered twice), IFSC lookup, and an optional name-match check.
  • Confirms & authorises with OTP; the payee enters a short cooling period.
  • Starts a transfer, picks the payee, enters the amount — balance & limits are checked.
  • Reviews a summary, authorises with OTP, and receives a UTR reference.
Outcome: everyday payments completed in chat, with bank-grade safeguards.
6

Open a deposit (FD / RD)

Existing Customer · investments
  • Chooses Fixed or Recurring Deposit and a source account.
  • Views live rates (senior-citizen rate applied automatically) and picks a tenure.
  • Sees a maturity preview with amount, date and TDS/penalty disclosures.
  • Confirms with OTP and receives the new deposit account number and advice.
Outcome: surplus funds invested instantly — new balances, deepened relationship.
7

Reach a human when needed

Either persona · escalation & feedback
  • Rates replies with a thumbs up/down as the conversation goes.
  • If the assistant senses it isn't helping, it proactively offers a representative.
  • Customer chooses to connect; the 24×7 helpline is surfaced.
Outcome: no dead ends — a graceful hand-off protects the experience.

Good To Know

Framing for the client

Where it stands: the assistant, multilingual voice, and all seven journeys are live and demonstrable today. The account-servicing and self-service journeys run against a demo core-banking layer, ready to connect to the bank's live systems during productionisation.

Please note: this is a working proof-of-concept. Rates, figures and the demo customer are illustrative; the security controls shown reflect the design intent and are finalised with the bank's compliance and engineering teams. Not in v1: new-account opening, ad-hoc transfers to unregistered accounts, UPI/bill-pay, and credit-card servicing.
